## What is original here

Everything in `assets/pv-*.js` and the verification tooling in `_pv/` is
original work, written for this project:

- the particle field engine and its form/force library
- the vector layer and its geometric constructions
- the score translation in `assets/pv-data.js` (timings measured from the
  recording; see "Measured audio facts" in the README)
- the procedurally generated artwork (`_pv/genart.js`, `assets/pv-art.js`)
- the fake-terminal and system layers

## Third-party data

**Natural Earth** — country outlines used for the six-nation sequence
(`assets/pv-geo.js`, generated by `_pv/mkgeo.js`).
Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

**Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 – Countries**, which is where the mainland and
island rings come from. Note that Taiwan is a separate feature in that dataset
and is explicitly merged into the China outline by `_pv/mkgeo.js`, because the
song's six-nation sequence draws each country as a single recognisable
silhouette.

## Tools this was built and verified with

- **Node.js** — runs the verification suite in `_pv/`
